Background: There is an concept in combat robots called "the thing". Builders refuse to use any other terminology to describe "the thing" becuase they fear mentioning "the thing" will cause your to lose a match because of it. For those not in the know (and at the risk of causing myself to becoming a victum), "the thing" is a position where your bot is staticly stable, but unable to drive. This opens you up to free hits from your competor or worse a count out due to lack of motion. No matter how unlikely it is that a robot will enter this postion, It will happen and it will kill your tournement run. My 30 pound robot Alan is at great risk of doing the thing. Due to to large weapon side relative to the wheels and weight distribution there are multiple orientations where I will get stuck if I get flipped, especially if my primary spinner is down. This project aims to increase the likelyhood that Alan will eventually land on their wheels by finding a better "ear" geometry. (Project name stolen from Jake Maximizer https://youtu.be/QvQso5vvPiM?si=-QEhTbfcA4jVenUB)

Methods: Using Mujoco I plan to simulate lots of inversion scenarios with various ear designs and use machine learning techniques to find a solution space ath will most like lead to Alan ending the right way up.
